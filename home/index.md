Hello! I'm a PhD student in computer science at Dartmouth College. My research is currently advised by [Dr. Brian Plancher](https://brianplancher.com/) in the [A²R Lab](https://a2r-lab.org/). I am grateful to have worked with [Dr. John Baillieul](https://sites.bu.edu/robotics/people/john-baillieul/) and [Dr. Jonathan Hurst](https://engineering.oregonstate.edu/about/people/jonathan-hurst) in the past.

I am interested in creating the equivalent of a motor cortex for our robotic systems! How should high-level reasoning interact with and become physical movement for embodied intelligence? I love movement and the beautiful expressions that can emerge from motion. My ultimate goal is to create a kind robot, like [BMO](https://adventuretime.fandom.com/wiki/BMO) in [Figure 1](https://siobhanchiffon.artstation.com/projects/4Vnll). Feel free to ask me more about what that actually means and entails!

In my free time, I'm an art history dilettante who enjoys reading, building robots, and spending as much quality time as I can with loved ones. Feel free to reach out if you want to chat!
